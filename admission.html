Admissions 2025–26

Give your child an education that inspires. Applications are now open across all grades — from Nursery to Grade 11. Follow our simple four-step process to begin the journey.

How It Works

The Admission Process

1

Enquire

Submit the online enquiry form or visit the admissions office to register your interest and receive the prospectus.

2

Apply

Complete the application form and upload the required documents — birth certificate, past records and photographs.

3

Assessment

Attend an interaction session or age-appropriate assessment along with a short parent orientation meeting.

4

Enrol

On selection, confirm the seat by paying the admission fee and completing the enrolment formalities.
